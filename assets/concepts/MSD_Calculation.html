A Step 1: Center of Mass & Unwrapping

Chain Viz

Why COM? A polymer chain wiggles (Rouse modes). If we track individual atoms, this wiggling looks like diffusion at short times ($t^{0.5}$). Calculating the Center of Mass (Red Dot) filters this noise, isolating the chain's net motion.

Why Unwrap? In MD, atoms warp across periodic boundaries (Pac-man style). We must "unwrap" these jumps to get a continuous trajectory $r(t)$ for calculating displacement.
